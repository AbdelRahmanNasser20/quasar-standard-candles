# Literature check: z > 6 quasars named in the draft (agent report, 2026-11-09)

Summary: 5 of 7 objects verified; J2346+0529 does not exist (SIMBAD/NED cone: highest-z QSO within 15' is z=3.06); J2239+0207 and J1205−0000 have no published JWST mass. Every original mass is Mg II-based. JWST Balmer masses agree with Mg II within the 0.4–0.5 dex systematics in every case checked.

Draft attribution errors: "Marshall et al. 2023" (A&A 678, A191) is about DELS J0411−0907 and VDES J0020−3653, not J1120; "Loiacono et al. 2024" (A&A 685, A121) is PJ308−21 at z=6.23, not J1120 (Hβ 2.7e9, Hα 1.9e9, Mg II 2.65e9, all within 0.5 dex); J0100 is in EIGER, not ASPIRE.

Standard estimator at z>6 = Mg II: Fan, Bañados & Simcoe 2023 ARA&A 61, 373 §4.1; Shen et al. 2019 ApJ 873, 35 ("fiducial BH mass estimates based on Mg II if available; otherwise C IV"); Farina et al. 2022 ApJ 941, 106 ("fiducial ... Mg II ... Shen et al. 2011 estimator"); Mazzucchelli et al. 2017 ApJ 849, 91.

JWST Hβ vs Mg II: Yang et al. 2023 (8 quasars): mean Hβ−Mg II = −0.13 dex, per-object −0.32 to +0.22 dex; Yue et al. 2024: Mg II masses "may be systematically larger by ~0.1 dex than their Hβ-based BH masses"; Eilers et al. 2023: consistent; Onoue et al. 2026 (arXiv:2609.09266): "overall agreement ... offsets of up to ~0.5 dex for individual objects".

"Overmassive" tension = M_BH/M_*: Pacucci et al. 2023 ApJL 957, L3 (BHs overmassive by 10–100× at fixed M_*, >3σ from local relation; seeds grow at 20–80 % Eddington); Maiolino et al. 2024 A&A 691, A145 (masses 4e5–8e7 M☉, overmassive relative to hosts, accretion <0.5 Eddington mostly).
