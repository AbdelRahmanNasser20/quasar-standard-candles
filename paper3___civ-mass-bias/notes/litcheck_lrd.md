# Literature check: Little Red Dots (agent report, 2026-11-09)

Bottom line: no LRD black-hole mass in any opened paper is C IV-based; all are Hα or Hβ virial masses. C IV is generally absent in LRDs (Rusakov et al.: He II, C IV, N V, [Ne V] absent in all 5/5 high-S/N sources with rest-UV coverage).

## Abell 2744-QSO1 (z = 7.045)
- Furtak et al. 2024, Nature (arXiv:2308.05735): M_BH ≈ 4 (+2/−1) ×10^7 M☉ from Hβ, FWHM 2800 ± 250 km/s. No C IV.
- Greene et al. 2024 (arXiv:2309.05714): MSA 13123 = QSO1, log M_BH = 7.3 ± 0.2 from Hα.
- D'Eugenio et al. 2025 (arXiv:2503.11752): Hα virial log M_BH = 7.2; Hα absorber at rest.
- Juodžbalis et al. 2025/2026 Nature (arXiv:2508.21748): DYNAMICAL mass ≈ 5×10^7 M☉, consistent with Balmer virial masses.
- Furtak et al. 2025 A&A 698 A227 (arXiv:2502.07875): variability consistent with 4×10^7 M☉.
- C IV only tentatively detected (Ji et al. 2026, arXiv:2604.03370, "O I, C IV and/or Fe II"); never used for a mass.

## "UNCOVER 29255": not found anywhere. Real UNCOVER broad-line masses (Greene et al. 2024, all Hα): 4286 z=5.84 logM=8.0; 13123 z=7.04 7.3; 13821 z=6.34 8.1; 20466 z=8.50 8.17 (Kokorev et al. 2023, arXiv:2308.11610, Hβ FWHM 3439 km/s); 23608 z=5.80 7.5; 35488 z=6.26 7.4; 38108 z=4.96 8.4; 41225 z=6.76 7.7; 45924 z=4.46 8.9 (Labbé et al. 2024, arXiv:2412.04557, Hα FWHM ≈ 4500 km/s).

## CEERS 1670 / 3210 (Kocevski et al. 2023, arXiv:2302.00012)
- CEERS 1670: z = 5.242, M_BH = 1.3 ± 0.4 ×10^7 M☉ from Hα (FWHM 2038 ± 286 km/s). CEERS 3210: z = 5.624, 0.9–4.7 ×10^7 M☉, Hα. No C IV.

## General
- LRDs are a JWST discovery (Matthee et al. 2024, arXiv:2306.05448, 20 broad-Hα emitters z≈4–6; Kocevski et al. 2024, arXiv:2404.03576, 341 LRDs).
- Rusakov et al., Nature 649, 574 (2026) (arXiv:2503.16595): Balmer lines broadened by electron scattering (τ_e 0.5–2.8); intrinsic cores give M_BH = 10^5–10^7 M☉, ~2 dex below previous estimates.
- Naidu et al. 2025 (arXiv:2503.16596) "black hole star"; de Graaff et al. 2025 (arXiv:2503.16600) "The Cliff"; Inayoshi & Maiolino 2025 ApJL (arXiv:2409.07805) dense-gas Balmer break.
- X-ray weakness: Yue et al. 2024 ApJL (arXiv:2404.13290) stack of 34 LRDs, limits ~1 dex (soft) / ~0.3 dex (hard) below L_X–L_Hα expectation; Ananna et al. 2024 ApJL (arXiv:2404.19010) no individual detections, ≥1.5 dex below virial-mass expectation; Maiolino et al. 2025 MNRAS 538, 1921 (arXiv:2405.00504) 71 JWST AGN, 1–2 dex X-ray weak.
- Non-virial widths: Lupi et al. 2024 A&A 689 A128 (arXiv:2406.17847); Lambrides et al. 2024 (arXiv:2409.13047); Pacucci & Narayan 2024 ApJ 976 (arXiv:2407.15915).
